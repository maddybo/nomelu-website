Privacy policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Nomelu is a local notes and tasks app. We do not collect data from the app.

Your notes and files

Notes, task details, attachments, history and settings are stored on your device or in folders you choose through the system Files picker. Nomelu does not upload your note contents to a server operated by us.

If you choose iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider handles storage and synchronization under its own privacy policy and terms.

No tracking or advertising

Nomelu includes no advertising, analytics SDKs or tracking. It does not require a Nomelu account and does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts or usage analytics.

Widgets and notifications

Widgets use a local cache shared between the app and its widget extension. Deadline notifications are scheduled locally and require your permission. Background refresh reads only sources you have chosen, when permitted by iOS.

Content from other services

Opening external links or loading remote images included in your notes can contact the services that host that content. Those services may receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address, under their own policies. Nomelu does not operate or control those services.

Support

If you email contact@ritualbyte.com, we use the information you provide to answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive note contents.

Deleting your information

You control your notes and chosen storage locations. Delete files through Nomelu or Files as appropriate; review the workspace trash and history when removing retained versions. Deleting Nomelu removes its private app data but does not necessarily delete files stored with external providers.

This website

This support website contains no analytics scripts or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes website requests under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions, contact contact@ritualbyte.com.
