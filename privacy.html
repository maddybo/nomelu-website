Privacy policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Your local notebooks stay in folders you choose. Optional Nomelu synchronization sends the notebooks you connect to our service so your devices and invited collaborators can work with them.

Local use

You can create, edit and organize local notes and tasks without a Nomelu account. Notes, task details, attachments, history, recovery drafts and appearance settings are stored on your device or in your chosen folders. Your display name, selected app color, avatar choice and default language are app preferences. They stay local when neither iCloud preferences nor a Nomelu account is in use.

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, when iCloud is available for Nomelu, the app keeps your display name, selected app color, avatar choice and default language in Apple’s iCloud key-value storage so they can be restored on your other Apple devices. These iCloud preferences do not require a Nomelu account. Onboarding can also discover your existing notebooks in Nomelu’s iCloud Drive container and download them when you restore them. If you choose iCloud Drive or another file provider for notebook files, that provider handles storage and file synchronization under its own privacy policy and terms.

Optional Nomelu synchronization and sharing

Signing in and connecting a notebook to Nomelu synchronization is optional. The native apps use https://sync.nomelu.com. Local notebooks that you do not connect are not uploaded to this service. When you sign in, your display name, selected app color and preset avatar are stored with your Nomelu account to keep your profile consistent across devices, including when no notebook is connected. The default language preference uses iCloud on Apple devices; it is not part of this Nomelu account profile.

When you use this service, it processes and stores:

  • Your account identifier, email address when provided, and the sign-in provider identifier needed to authenticate you.
  • Your display name, selected app color and preset avatar choice for account profile synchronization.
  • Registered device identifiers and names, session records and synchronization cursors.
  • The connected notebooks' names, notes, tasks, task descriptions, custom properties, images and other attachments, along with change records needed to synchronize them.
  • Sharing permissions and invitations, including a recipient email address when you choose an email-restricted invitation.
  • Login challenges and limited abuse-prevention counters. Network services receive ordinary request information, such as an IP address, when a connection is made.

We use this information to provide sign-in, synchronization, sharing, access control and service security. Your notebook content is not used for advertising or tracking. Nomelu synchronization uses HTTPS for transfers but is not end-to-end encrypted: synchronized content is stored on the service. People you invite can receive copies according to the permission you grant.

Email sign-in and emailed invitations use our configured email delivery provider. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, that provider processes the sign-in under its own terms and privacy policy. Nomelu receives the account information that provider returns; it does not receive your Apple or Google password.

No advertising or cross-app tracking

Nomelu includes no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not use your data for cross-app tracking. It does not request your location or contacts. Optional synchronization account and device records are used for app functionality and security, not to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Share extension, widgets and notifications

The iPhone/iPad Share extension copies content you choose into a private inbox shared with Nomelu. The app then imports it into your selected note, task or new note. Content is sent to the synchronization service only if its destination notebook is connected. The extension does not hold notebook access bookmarks or server session tokens.

Widgets use a local cache shared with the app. Deadline notifications are scheduled locally and require your permission. Background refresh reads your chosen sources and may synchronize connected notebooks when the operating system permits.

External content and integrations

Opening links, fetching link previews or loading remote images can contact the services hosting that content. They may receive the URL requested and ordinary network information, including your IP address, under their own policies. Nomelu does not control those services.

If you enable an integration or give another application access to a notebook, that application handles the information you make available under its own privacy policy. Review its permissions before granting access.

Deleting your information

You control your local files and chosen storage locations. Review notebook trash and history when removing retained local versions. Deleting the app removes its private data according to the operating system, but does not necessarily delete files held by external providers or copies on other devices.

Signing out stops account synchronization on that device and clears its local session; it does not delete your server account or notebooks. Disconnecting a notebook makes that local folder independent without deleting the server notebook or other people's copies.

To remove your synchronization account, use Settings → Danger zone → Delete sync account and server data. This removes your account, synchronized account profile and notebooks you own from the synchronization service. Changes already shared into notebooks owned by other people can remain with the deleted account's identity removed. Your local files, copies already held by other people, and preferences or notebook files held in iCloud are not deleted by this action.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact contact@ritualbyte.com, we use the information you send to answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive note contents. You can use the same address for questions about account data, access or deletion.

This website

This support website contains no analytics scripts or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes website requests under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We update this policy when the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
